Live Placement Drift Experiments (KinD)

Real disruptions on a local KinD cluster; the Kubernetes scheduler decides placement and the Component 2 model observes T0 and T1. Results are read from the stored run files, so no cluster is needed to view them.

ℹ️ What am I looking at?
  • Each experiment: deploy 4 services × 3 replicas → snapshot (T0) → one real Kubernetes action → snapshot (T1) → compare.
  • Only paymentservice is disrupted; all 4 services are observed. Each experiment ran 5 times, with and without a spread rule.
  • The services are lightweight stand-ins (pause image): they test where Kubernetes places pods, not the application itself.
  • Click a row to see where the pods were before and after (first run).
